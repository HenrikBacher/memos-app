An Android client for memos (https://usememos.com) — the self-hosted, lightweight note-taking server. Bring your own server URL and access token; your notes stay on infrastructure you control.

Features:
• Browse, search, and tag-filter your memos with an offline-first cache.
• Markdown editing with live preview, attachments, and visibility controls (private / protected / public).
• Time-based reminders that fire as system notifications, even when the app isn't running.
• Share-target integration: send text from any app straight to a new memo.
• Home-screen widget for one-tap quick-note creation.
• Material 3 design, dynamic color, light and dark themes.

Requires a running memos server (v0.22+) and an access token from Settings → My Account.
